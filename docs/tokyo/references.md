## Palette and composition

- Day: cyan sky, ivory clouds, lavender shadow planes, cream concrete; signs add restrained vermilion and teal.
- Sunset: violet → pink → apricot sky, low flat sun, warm building faces and plum shadows.
- Night: navy → indigo sky, moon and sparse stars, mixed warm/cool windows, local cyan/magenta neon.
- Keep the center behind the focused CD quieter. Place the tower off-center and giant screens toward the edges.
- Japanese sign copy: 音楽, レコード, カラオケ, ゲーム, CD, ラジオ, ダンス, 渋谷. Production text should be drawn deterministically after the Japanese font loads.
